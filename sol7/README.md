# sol7 — model-based planner + Hungarian labor (no tuned strategy vector)

**Submission:** `56275780` (2026-09-16). sol7 is the first solution that is **not** a CEM-tuned
heuristic vector. It keeps sol5's proven mechanics layer (feeder routes, feeding guarantee,
shed logistics, atomic-plant rule, SELL-first ordering, ≤10 orders) and replaces the strategy
with three components derived from the engine's own rules:

1. **DailyPlanner** (hour 0): reads `obs.town.unlocked_shops` → per-product sink (units/day)
   plus the expectation of the remaining random draws; reads the opponent's public tiles →
   their production capacity per day, calibrated by their observed sales (market delta +
   town consumption − our own sales). Computes the marginal *season* value of one more cow /
   sheep / goose / strawberry tile by simulating that product's market inventory to day 29
   with the engine price curve, net of purchase, feed and labor opportunity cost, plus the
   revenue our flow removes from the opponent. Buys while positive, culls (stops feeding)
   animals whose remaining value is negative.
2. **Hungarian assignment** for the crop crew (optimal unit↔task matching instead of the
   greedy nearest-unit sweep): walk share 0.589 → 0.559, +7.5k paired margin vs sol5.
3. **Inventory-targeting sell rule** for STRAWBERRY/MILK/WOOL/TOMATO/CARROT: sell down to I0
   on the scarcity side, one consumption tick of residual sink above it, a reservation
   fraction decaying 0.9 → 0.25 from day 10, exit first if the opponent's flow ≥ 0.9×sink,
   liquidate if the excess cannot drain before the end. MELON/EGG/WHEAT/FERTILIZER keep
   sol5's rules.

Hands: 6 (d0-3), 9 (d4-7), 11 (d8-28), 8 (d29). Constants are engine-derived plus one labor
opportunity cost ($25/action) and the reservation decay.
